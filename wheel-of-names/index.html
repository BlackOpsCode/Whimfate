THE PRACTICAL BIT

A little spin.
A clear answer.

Whimfate's free Wheel of Names is a simple way to pick one name or option from a list. Add the entries you want on the wheel, spin it, and let the pointer land on the selection. It works as a random name picker, a choice wheel, or a quick decision wheel whenever a group needs one answer.

Use a random picker wheel to choose who goes first, select a classroom helper, pick a game-night activity, decide which team presents, choose a movie, or give every option a place in a friendly draw. You can enter people’s names, places, tasks, prizes, ideas, or any other short options. Keep one entry on each line so the wheel is easy to review before you spin.

The wheel is useful when there are several equally acceptable choices and talking about them is taking longer than doing them. It gives the group a visible selection instead of another round of “I don't mind” or “you pick”. For repeated turns, enable Remove winner after each spin so a selected entry is taken out before the next round. This is handy for classroom turn-taking, selecting volunteers, party games and other situations where you want each entry to be picked once.

Every line represents one entry on the wheel. If a name or option appears more than once, each occurrence has its own slice, so repeated entries are included separately. Review the list before spinning, especially when using the wheel for a draw with a group. You can share the selected result as text, send the result picture on devices that support image sharing, or download a branded image to post in a group chat.

The Wheel of Names is free to use and does not ask you to create an account. Add a list, spin when you're ready, and use the result as a quick starting point for your next decision. The wheel can pick the name; what happens next is between you and the group chat.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before the wheel starts spinning.

How does the Wheel of Names choose a winner?

Add the names or options you want included and spin the wheel. Each entry receives a slice, and the wheel stops with one entry selected as the result. The selected name appears below the wheel, ready to copy or share.

Can I use it as a random name picker?

Absolutely. Enter one name per line to pick a student, volunteer, player, presenter or first turn. You can also use words or short phrases instead of names, which makes the wheel useful for activities, topics and everyday choices.

Can I remove a winner after each spin?

Yes. Turn on Remove winner after each spin before spinning. The selected entry is removed from the remaining wheel, so the next spin chooses from the entries left. Turn the option off when you want every entry to stay available for every spin.

Do repeated names have a higher chance of being picked?

Each line is treated as a separate entry. If the same name appears twice, it has two slices on the wheel. This is useful when you intentionally want an option to appear more than once, so check the list if every person should have only one entry.

Can I use the wheel for classrooms, games and giveaways?

It can be used for classroom activities, turn-taking, game nights, choosing a volunteer, picking a team representative, deciding what to play or selecting one option from a casual list. For formal prize draws, check the rules that apply to your event and make sure participants understand how entries are included.

Can I share the selected name with my group?

Use Share text to send the selected name and link, Share picture on devices that support direct image sharing, or Download image to save the result card as a PNG and attach it to WhatsApp, Discord or another group chat.

Are my entries saved or uploaded?

You can use the wheel without an account. The entries are used in your browser for the current session and are not submitted through a name-entry form to a Whimfate account.
