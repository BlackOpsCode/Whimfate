WHY WHIMFATE?

Less “you choose”.
More actually doing it.

Choosing something should not be the hardest part of doing it. But a simple question — what should we eat, who goes first, how do we split the teams? — can turn into an endless loop of suggestions, vetoes and “I'm fine with anything.” Whimfate is being built for exactly those moments: lightweight randomizers and funny decision games that turn a stuck conversation into a next step.

Use a random team generator when you need groups for a game night, classroom activity, sports session or office icebreaker. Flip a virtual coin when two options feel equally good. Roll dice to add chance to a tabletop game. Use a random number picker for a quick draw, or put your options on a decision wheel when nobody wants to be the person who chooses dinner. The stakes stay small; the relief can be surprisingly real.

Whimfate is designed to be straightforward: no account to create, no elaborate setup, and no lecture about productivity. The aim is to make simple random choices fast, clear and fun — with results that are easy to show a friend or share with the group. Team Splitter, Coin Flip and Dice Roller are playable now. The next tools in the maybe pile are a daily oracle, quick either-or picks, random numbers and a decision wheel.

GOOD QUESTIONS

The essentials.

What is Whimfate?

Whimfate is a collection of simple randomizers and playful decision games for everyday choices: splitting a group into teams, flipping a coin, rolling dice, choosing between two options, or picking a number. Team Splitter, Coin Flip and Dice Roller are playable now, with more little games being added one at a time.

Can I use a random team splitter for games, school or work?

Yes. Team Splitter is ready for game nights, classrooms, sports, workshops and team activities where you want to distribute names quickly without hand-picking sides.

Will the Whimfate tools be free?

The project is being built around free, easy-to-use tools. No account is required for the current website. Individual features will be described clearly as they launch.

Does randomness make a decision fair?

It can make a low-stakes choice more impartial when the options are already acceptable. It cannot make an unfair set of options fair, and it should not replace careful judgment for important, consequential decisions.

Does Whimfate decide my big life choices?

Please do not let a virtual coin decide whether to move countries or text your ex. Whimfate is for tiny dilemmas, casual games and breaking harmless deadlocks — the important decisions are still yours.
