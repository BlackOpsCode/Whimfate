Coin flip FAQ

Is this online coin flip random?

The result is selected independently of the animation, using crypto.getRandomValues() when the browser supports it and a standard random fallback otherwise. The coin's final face is then animated to match the selected result.

Can I share the coin flip result as an image?

Yes. Download a high-resolution PNG card with the coin, result, a rotating funny line, and the wimfate.com watermark. On devices that support sharing files, Share picture can attach the image directly on supported devices; otherwise use Download image and attach the PNG manually.

Does Whimfate store my choices?

No account is needed. Flip history and counts exist in the current page session only; the module does not send your choices or names to a server.
