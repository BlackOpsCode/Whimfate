A SIMPLE WAY TO MAKE A PICK

Two options. One less decision.

This or That is a free two-option random decision maker. Enter the two choices you are considering, press the button, and get one randomly selected result. It can be used for everyday decisions, quick group choices, friendly challenges, game nights, classroom activities, or any situation where you want chance to choose between two alternatives.

Unlike a question deck or a quiz, this tool does not require preset topics or prepared dilemmas. You supply both options yourself: what to eat, which film to watch, where to go, which task to do first, who starts a board game, or two completely unrelated ideas. The optional context field helps make the result easier to understand when you share it.

Use the result as a lighthearted tie-breaker, not as a substitute for important personal, medical, legal, or financial judgment. Your options and recent decisions stay in the current browser session. No account is needed, and you can share a text verdict or a branded result image with friends.

QUICK ANSWERS

This or That FAQ

How does the This or That decision maker work?

Enter one choice in Option A and another in Option B, then press Let Fate Decide. The tool randomly selects one of the two options and displays it as the result.

Can I enter any two options?

Yes. Add any pair of options you want: meals, places, films, activities, names, chores, game-night choices, or something entirely different. The optional situation field can describe what the decision is about.

Is the choice really random?

The tool uses a random bit from the browser's Web Crypto API when it is available. A standard browser-random fallback is used otherwise. The animation is only a visual reveal and does not influence which option is selected.

Can I use it to settle a group decision?

Yes. Enter the two options everyone is considering, make the selection, and share the result as text or as an image. It can be a quick way to settle low-stakes choices in a group chat or at a game table.

Does Whimfate store the options I enter?

No account is required. The options and recent decisions are processed in your browser and are not sent to a Whimfate server by this tool.

Can I share the result on WhatsApp or Discord?

Use Share text to send the decision and page link, Share picture to send the PNG through your device's share menu where supported, or Download image to attach it manually in WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, or another app.
