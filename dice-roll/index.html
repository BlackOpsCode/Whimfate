A QUICK WORD BEFORE INITIATIVE

Virtual dice for tabletop games and everyday decisions

Whimfate is a free online dice roller for tabletop RPG sessions, board games, classroom activities, probability demos and all the tiny disputes that require a number. Choose a four-sided die (D4), six-sided die (D6), D8, D10, D12, D20 or D100, then roll one die or several at once.

Each roll shows its individual results and the combined total, so you can check damage, initiative, random tables, challenges or a quick game of chance without installing an app. The roll happens in your browser, and the recent-roll history stays on this visit rather than being saved to an account.

THE QUESTIONS, BEFORE THE QUEST

Dice roller FAQ

Which dice can I roll?

You can roll D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and D100. Choose the type before rolling.

Can I roll more than one die?

Yes. Roll up to eight dice together. Whimfate displays each die result and adds them into one total.

Is this online dice roller free?

Yes. It runs in your browser without an account or an installation.

Can I share a roll with my group?

Use Share text, send a branded PNG where your device supports image sharing, or download the card and attach it to your group chat.
