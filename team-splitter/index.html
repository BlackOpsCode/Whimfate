THE PRACTICAL BIT

Fair teams.
Less committee.

Whimfate's free random team generator splits a list of names into balanced groups in seconds. Paste players, classmates, colleagues or a game-night crew, choose how many teams you need, and let the shuffle do the awkward part.

The Team Splitter shuffles the list locally in your browser and deals people across the teams so group sizes differ by no more than one. If there are 11 people and 3 teams, two teams will have 4 people and one team will have 3. The draw doesn't upload or save your list to a server.

Use it for classroom activities, sports, board games, workshops, office icebreakers or any situation where someone is about to start picking friends first. Use Share text to send the team list, Share picture on devices that support direct image sharing, or Download image and attach the PNG to the group chat.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before someone objects.

How does the random team generator work?

The tool uses the browser's cryptographic random-number generator when available to shuffle the names, then distributes them across the selected teams. The number of people in each team will differ by at most one.

Can I use it for school, sports or work?

Yes. The Team Splitter works for casual games, classroom groups, sports practice, workshops, office activities and other low-stakes situations where you want a quick, neutral starting point.

Are my names uploaded anywhere?

No. The split happens in your browser. This module has no account, database or server endpoint for the names you enter. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into public computers.

Can teams be exactly the same size?

Only when the number of people divides evenly by the number of teams. Otherwise, some teams will have one extra person. This tool keeps that difference to one person and randomizes the assignment.
