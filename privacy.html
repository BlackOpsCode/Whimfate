PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Your data isn't the main character.

Privacy should be straightforward, not hidden behind a maze of legal language.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 25, 2026

This policy describes the intended privacy approach for Whimfate (the “Site”). It should be updated whenever the live site adds analytics, advertising, accounts, server-side processing, or other material data practices. This is a general policy draft, not legal advice.

Information you provide

The current foundation does not ask you to register or provide personal details. If you contact the team through a channel you choose, the information in that message will be handled for the purpose of responding to you.

How the tools are designed to work

Whimfate is being designed with browser-first tools in mind. For simple randomizers and decision helpers, the intended approach is to process your inputs in your browser wherever practical. Do not enter sensitive personal information into a public website tool unless a tool clearly explains why it needs the information and how it is handled.

Some technical data may be processed by the hosting provider when you load the Site, such as information needed to deliver pages securely and reliably. The exact records retained depend on the hosting configuration and provider terms.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The foundation version does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies through its own code. Hosting infrastructure, browser features, or third-party services may behave differently depending on deployment. If analytics, advertising partners, affiliate links, or consent-dependent technologies are added, this policy should be revised to identify the relevant services and explain available choices before that deployment goes live.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell information that you type into a tool. We may rely on infrastructure providers to host and deliver the Site. If a feature introduces a third-party service or sends information outside your browser, that feature should explain the change clearly.

Children

The Site is a general-purpose set of casual tools, not a service designed to collect information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online services as appropriate.

Your choices

You can stop using the Site at any time. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, though blocking certain technologies may affect how a website functions. Requests about personal information can be directed to the contact channel listed on the live Site, once one is published.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Whimfate grows. The updated date at the top of the page will indicate when the text was last revised. Material changes should be reflected here before or when the relevant feature launches.

Important deployment note

Before enabling ad networks or analytics in production, review this policy against the actual hosting and vendor setup, and add any legally required consent controls. This page is a starting point and must match the real implementation.
